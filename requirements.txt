# never changes, and an unpinned dependency means a redeploy in six months

# differently. These ranges allow bug fixes and block surprises.
#
# After any upgrade, run:  python verify.py <csv> "<vessel>" <reference.docx>
# That is the real safeguard - the pins just stop it happening by accident.
